## Reported by the owner on this set

The paths in [../01-on-device-settings.md](../01-on-device-settings.md) for
VIDAA U6 were present with the documented names, and the owner switched the
settings off:

- Settings → Advanced settings → Enhanced viewing settings → Enable Automatic
  Content Recognition → Off, with the "No, thank you" and "Yes, Decline"
  pop-ups.
- Device usage data and App usage data collection, in the same area.
- Settings → System → Advanced settings → Personalised Ads → Off, and Reset
  Ads ID.
- Settings → Support → Disclaimer and Privacy → EULA and Other Policy and
  Services: data-sharing consents withdrawn.

After the pass, the owner reported apps, HDMI inputs, YouTube and Netflix
working normally.
